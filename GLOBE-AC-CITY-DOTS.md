# Globe + Geographic Index

The Institutions globe now displays only thin latitude/longitude lines, a spherical silhouette, and dots for cities represented in the archive. No country borders, no national territories, no solid land areas. Country and city names are available through the adjacent geographic index, with filters in the original institution list below. Dot locations use city-center coordinates for navigation, not precise institution addresses. Drag, zoom, reset, keyboard navigation and city-dot click remain. Other site pages are unchanged.

Requires WebGL. Astro build and in-browser interaction have not been verified in this environment.

14:00–14:50   MUTEK.JP Pro Conference

Exploring New Possibilities for Creative Collaboration and International Cultural Exchange at the Intersection of Art, Music, and Technology: This special session brings together leading cultural professionals from the UK to explore how technology is transforming music and visual art, and the new creative possibilities emerging when artists engage with technology.

The discussion will also examine opportunities for cross-border collaboration, the development of artist communities, and how international networks can support the future of arts and culture.

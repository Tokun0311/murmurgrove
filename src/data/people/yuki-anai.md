Yuki Anai is the founder and CEO of non-classic and a media artist. Born in Oita, Japan, he earned a Master's degree from Keio University Graduate School of Media Design. While still a student, he launched a digital art team at teamLab and directed live productions for established artists.

In 2016 he founded non-classic, creating large-scale spatial productions such as Yorunoyo (Yokohama), the autumn illumination at Nikko Toshogu Shrine, and Meyameya (Kobe). His work has been presented at Ars Electronica (Austria) and the Athens Digital Arts Festival (Greece). In 2026, his new work “Passage of Time” was permanently installed in the atrium of the Oita Prefectural Art Museum (OPAM) to mark the museum’s 10th anniversary.

X Zhu-Nowell is a curator, writer, and institutional practitioner, currently serving as the executive director and chief curator of the Rockbund Art Museum, Shanghai. Zhu-Nowell’s practice is shaped by relational authorship and an approach to exhibition making that foregrounds contradiction, displacement, and the labor of (mis)translation. At RAM, they have led a structural shift toward a resistant institution by advancing artist commissions, research-led practices, and radical pedagogies through initiatives such as AUUUUDITORIUM and Complex Geographies. Recent projects at RAM include “Irena Haiduk: Nula” (2025–26), which transformed the museum into an active film set and participatory economy, and “The Great Camouflage” (2025–26), an exhibition cocurated with Kandis Williams. Prior to joining RAM, Zhu-Nowell was a curator at the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, New York (2014–21), where they organized “Wu Tsang: Anthem,” which was named one of the best exhibitions of 2021 by the New York Times.

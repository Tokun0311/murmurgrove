# Wireframe globe update

- Country paths are drawn as vector-derived strokes, with no land-area fill.
- Fine 30-degree latitude and longitude lines are computed directly on the globe.
- Countries represented in the institution archive have a slightly stronger green outline.
- White background, smaller floating sphere, no rectangular panel.
- Rotation, keyboard controls, zoom, reset, country popups and institution filters remain.
- Existing project content, People A-Z, hidden hamster and Writing are unchanged.
- WebGL is required. Reduced-motion preference is respected by avoiding autonomous rotation.

Murmurgrove V6.2

Changes in this update:
1. Removed automatic label rendering at high zoom. Record titles now appear only via hover/focus tooltip.
2. Added growth-responsive morphology for concepts, institutions, and people.
   - Concepts enlarge into clustered cellular forms as their directional weight and linked content increase.
   - Institutions thicken into anchored bodies with stronger outer structure as related records increase.
   - People become paired / bridging forms and grow with linked activity.
3. Writing and photography remain quieter supporting record forms.
4. Existing V6.1 interaction fixes remain unchanged:
   - zoom to pointer
   - no free panning drag
   - reset to centre at 100%
   - maximum zoom 1000%

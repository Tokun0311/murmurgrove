# Murmurgrove · Becoming II

## Sonic relationship with TOW ARCHIVE

This prototype replaces the previous threshold-triggered sound events with an aperiodic, continuously evolving, non-tonal acoustic field.

**Archive source**: the four Astro collections are joined into records and explicit or repeated exhibition associations by `src/lib/murmurgrove.ts`. The 3D homepage translates those relations into positions and connected root trajectories. Every ~250 ms *while the user has chosen to play sound*, the homepage samples the same world-space moving record positions and root endpoints (not merely visual pixels).

The resulting bounded metrics are:

- `coherence`: alignment of sampled record velocity vectors.
- `tension`: mean related-record root separation plus movement mismatch.
- `transformation`: change in sampled velocity vectors between observations.
- `persistence`: root strength and direct/shared relation reinforcement.
- `differentiation`: lack of alignment and lack of root-relative coordination.
- `integration`: similarity of velocities at connected root endpoints.
- `emergence`: short-term change in root separation tension plus transformation.

No metric is a measure of record count, nor is one record one sound. The calculation samples a bounded number of points and roots for mobile performance. The audio module low-pass integrates these parameters at different time scales (2.1–17.8 seconds) and changes three overlapping, filtered, non-tonal sound fields; a persistent *lineage* state drifts across the listening session. Neither musical scales, chords, event clocks, random sample selection, recordings nor simulated seedlings are used. The recorded mangrove event is a **conceptual reference** about irreversible becoming, not an audio source.

**History**: Selecting an archived state changes the inputs to the same renderer and reshapes the sonic state. This is *not* a recording of an earlier year. The new `sonicVersion` is `sonic-becoming-ii-v1`. Long-term sonic lineage snapshots are not yet saved.

**Accessibility**: audio defaults OFF; sound starts only after a user gesture, fades in/out, and suspends when the tab is hidden. The original 3D interactivity, controls, zoom and content links remain intact.

**Limitations**: the browser audio is synthesized and the sound quality must be judged by listening. The 250ms metric stream cannot represent very fast sub-frame interactions. Continuous non-tonal synthesis may still be perceived as electronic. This version is not a claim of physical biophony or biological simulation, and does not change the underlying archive data.

# Murmurgrove Phase 2.6.4: Interior Quiet Records & Particle Focus

## Intent
An unlinked record is **not excluded** from the organism and it is not a failed research node. It can occupy a locally independent interval of space, without a fabricated relationship.

## Read-only spatial policy
- Existing 181-record frozen knowledge baseline and T0 projection remain byte-identical.
- 174 records participate in the verified viewer; exactly 158 metadata relationships remain actual drawn links.
- 20 records that are disconnected **in the non-quarantined projection** receive stable, deterministic *render-only* positions in lacunae inside the 123-node main body rather than the previous artificial outer shell.
- The source `position` and `displayPosition` inside T0.json are unchanged; the interior visual placement is an **interpretive display arrangement, not a historical or scientific claim**.
- Independent records have small bounded, ID-seeded asynchronous optical drift. They are excluded from neighbor coupling and from transferred observational pulse waves. No new metadata edge, life event, threshold work, Writing core, or physiological status is created.

## Desktop and mobile click contract
1. Click or tap an actual particle. Camera eases toward its stable local resting position and automatically reaches at least 165% zoom, while the chosen particle continues its bounded optical movement.
2. An anchored minimal label appears. **Only the record title** is the primary link to its actual TOW ARCHIVE page; the role line and panel surface do not navigate.
3. Zoom can reach **2400%** to inspect one particle's local motion. When following an isolated node beyond 800%, unrelated strands visually recede; beyond 1200% unrelated traveling glints are suppressed. These are presentation-only reductions, not data mutations.
4. At 200%+, the small locator lets you return to the overview. Zooming back to <=100% also progressively recenters the body.
5. The current camera, selection, view and normalized pan remain in sessionStorage and can be restored after browser Back.
